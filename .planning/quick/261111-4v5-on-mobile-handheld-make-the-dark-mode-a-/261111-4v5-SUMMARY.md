# Quick Task 261111-4v5: Mobile Handheld Dark Mode Toggle & White Default Summary

**Defaulted first-time visitors to crisp white/light mode with zero pre-paint flash, added single-tap Light/Dark toggles to the Mobile Handheld header and profile, and fixed light/dark contrast across all `/driver/*` views and Leaflet maps.**

## Accomplishments

- **Zero-Flash White Default & Meta Sync (`client/index.html`, `ThemeContext.tsx`):** Updated `<meta name="theme-color">` to `#FFFFFF` and added a synchronous `<head>` bootstrap script reading `localStorage.getItem('smartroute-theme')` so first-time users resolve immediately to `data-theme="light"`, `colorScheme = 'light'`, and `#FFFFFF` theme-color with no `.dark` class. Extended `ThemeContext` with `toggleTheme()`, initial `resolveEffectiveTheme()`, `root.style.colorScheme`, and live `meta[name="theme-color"]` synchronization (`#0B0C0E` dark / `#FFFFFF` light).
- **Mobile-Ergonomic Light/Dark Toggles (`ThemeToggle.tsx`, `DriverHeader.tsx`, `DriverProfilePage.tsx`):** Added `variant="toggle"` (compact `w-8 h-8` single-tap Sun/Moon button) and `variant="switch"` (`role="switch"` 44x24px pill switch with `"Light"` / `"Dark"` mono label) while keeping `'segmented'` and `'icon-only'` intact. Replaced the crowded 3-option segmented control in `DriverHeader` with `<ThemeToggle variant="toggle" />` and tightened header spacing for 360px+ phones; updated `DriverProfilePage` appearance row to `"Dark Mode"` (`"White daylight mode by default"`) with `<ThemeToggle variant="switch" />` and dark-mode-safe emerald/amber/red classes.
- **Driver Handheld & Leaflet Map Contrast Parity (`DriverRoutePage.tsx`, `DriverSupportPage.tsx`, `CurrentStopHeroCard.tsx`, `DriverVehicleStatusCard.tsx`, `PODModal.tsx`, `InteractiveMap.tsx`):** Added `dark:` contrast variants to badges, call buttons, drawer rows, incident notices, Skip controls, and End Shift button; added `border-outline-variant/60` dividers, `text-on-surface` inputs, and explicit `bg-white` + `#0F172A` ink on `PODModal`'s signature pad; keyed Leaflet `<TileLayer>` on `isDark` for instant tile swapping and replaced hardcoded slate colors inside `<Tooltip>` and `<Popup>` with semantic theme tokens.
