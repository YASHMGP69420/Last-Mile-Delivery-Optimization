Implement the multi-depot vehicle routing (MDVRP) logic and nearest-hub consignment clustering. Enable delivery batches to cluster consignments to their geographically closest active warehouse or receive a dispatcher override, and ensure that route optimization and dispatch calculations strictly enforce a closed-loop tour topology departing from and returning to the exact assigned origin hub.

Purpose: Eliminate single-depot bottlenecks by distributing consignments across regional hubs and calculating realistic closed-loop travel distances and drive times back to the origin facility.
Output: Hub clustering service, multi-depot batch controller updates, closed-loop routing logic, and comprehensive MDVRP test suite.
</objective>
