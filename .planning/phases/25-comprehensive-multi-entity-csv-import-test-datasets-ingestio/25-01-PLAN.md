# Plan 25-01: Comprehensive Multi-Entity CSV Import Test Datasets & Ingestion Validation

## Objective
Generate comprehensive, production-ready, high-density sample CSV test files explicitly named according to what they represent (`orders.csv`, `drivers.csv`, `vehicles.csv`). Ensure all phone numbers are non-real test numbers, all data strictly conforms to backend schema and business validation rules, and provide 1-click template downloads in the UI.

       - Headers: `orderReference,customerName,customerPhone,customerEmail,street,city,zone,postalCode,weightKg,volumeM3,priority,timeWindowStart,timeWindowEnd,serviceDurationMinutes,latitude,longitude`
       - Order references: `#OD-BHUB-1001` to `#OD-BHUB-1050`.
       - Non-real test phone numbers: `+91 99999 00001` through `+91 99999 00050` (valid 10-15 digit formatting, strictly non-real phone series).
       - Accurately distributed across all 5 zones (`Patia (Z-1)`, `Jaydev Vihar (Z-2)`, `Saheed Nagar (Z-3)`, `Khandagiri (Z-4)`, `Chandrasekharpur`, `Other`), valid pincodes, priorities (`Standard`, `Urgent`, `Cold Chain`), and Bhubaneswar geo-coordinates (lat ~20.25 to 20.35, lng ~85.80 to 85.87).
    3. Author `drivers.csv` with exactly 20 rows of commercial driver profiles:
       - Headers: `name,email,phone,licenseNumber,password`
       - Names: realistic Indian full names.
       - Emails: `driver.test01@smartroute.internal` to `driver.test20@smartroute.internal`.
       - Non-real test phone numbers: `+91 91111 00001` through `+91 91111 00020`.
       - Unique commercial license numbers: `OD-02-DL-2024-101` through `OD-02-DL-2024-120`.
       - Standard test password: `driver123`.
    4. Author `vehicles.csv` with exactly 20 rows of diverse vehicles:
       - Headers: `vehicleId,makeModel,type,capacityKg,capacityM3,fuelType,kmPerLitre,energyKWhPerKm,tankCapacityLitres,lastOdometerKm,availability`
       - Vehicle IDs: `OD-02-TX-1001` through `OD-02-TX-1020`.
       - Covering `Bike`, `Van`, and `Truck` models (Tata Ace Gold, Treo Zor EV, Hero Electric Nyx, Mahindra Bolero, Ashok Leyland Dost, Eicher Pro 2049, BharatBenz).
       - Fuel types: `Diesel`, `Petrol`, `Electric` with strictly valid combustion vs electric efficiency numbers (e.g., Electric has `energyKWhPerKm > 0` and empty `kmPerLitre`, Diesel has `kmPerLitre > 0` and empty `energyKWhPerKm`).
    5. Replicate the identical CSV files into `client/public/sample_data/` so they can be downloaded directly from the frontend via browser static asset routing (`/sample_data/orders.csv`, `/sample_data/drivers.csv`, `/sample_data/vehicles.csv`).
  </action>
