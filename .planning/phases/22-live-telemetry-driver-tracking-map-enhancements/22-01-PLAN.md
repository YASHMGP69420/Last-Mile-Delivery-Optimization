<objective>
Deliver the foundational Live Driver Navigation Marker and Camera Navigation Engine (TELEM-MAP-01, TELEM-MAP-02).

Purpose: In the current Fleet Tracking view, vehicle GPS locations are injected with synthetic numeric sequence numbers (sequenceOrder: idx + 1), causing drivers to be indistinguishable from delivery stops and labeled with a confusing numeric "1" pin. Furthermore, dispatchers have no camera targeting controls to center on an active driver. This plan creates a dedicated pulsing navigation arrowhead oriented by heading with a real-time driver name & speed badge pill, removes numeric stop pin overloading, adds a dedicated "Find Driver" camera flyTo action (zoom level 16) from both the map canvas and sidebar vehicle cards, and introduces an auto-follow engine that gracefully detaches upon manual map pan.

Output:
- RouteStop interface extended with driver telemetry fields in `client/src/types/route.ts`
- MockLeaflet extended with `divIcon` in `client/src/test/setup.ts`
- SVG/HTML marker generator `createDriverIcon` in `client/src/components/maps/markerIcons.ts`
- Programmatic camera controller `CameraController.tsx`
- InteractiveMap and TrackingPage integrated with live driver navigation markers and camera navigation
- Automated test suites in `driverMarker.test.tsx` and `cameraController.test.tsx`
</objective>
