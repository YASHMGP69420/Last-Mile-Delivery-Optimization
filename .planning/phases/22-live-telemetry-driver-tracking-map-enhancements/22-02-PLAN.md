<objective>
Deliver Status-Differentiated Stop Pins, Dual-Color Route Segmentation, and Collapsible Filterable Map Legend (TELEM-MAP-03, TELEM-MAP-04).

Purpose: In the current mapping interface, all delivery stops compete equally for visual attention with identical large green pins, obscuring active priorities and cluttering high-density delivery routes. Completed deliveries look identical to pending ones, cancelled orders lack clear alert markers, and route paths do not differentiate between past journey and upcoming commitments. This plan introduces distinct status styling (dimmed flat grey dots for completed, bold red slash pins for cancelled/failed, pulsing bright indigo for the next active stop, and vibrant emerald for pending deliveries), splits route polylines into traversed (muted dashed) and remaining (solid emerald) segments, and adds a floating glassmorphic map legend with interactive category filter checkboxes.

Output:
- Enhanced stop pin generator `createStatusStopIcon` in `client/src/components/maps/markerIcons.ts`
- Glassmorphic overlay component `MapLegend.tsx` with category filter checkboxes
- InteractiveMap updated with dual polyline rendering and marker visibility filtering
- TrackingPage updated to compute traversed vs remaining route paths
- Automated test suites in `statusPinIcons.test.tsx` and `mapLegendFilters.test.tsx`
</objective>
