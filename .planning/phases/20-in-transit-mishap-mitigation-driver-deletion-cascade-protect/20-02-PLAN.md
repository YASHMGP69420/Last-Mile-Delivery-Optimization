<objective>
Implement the frontend user-facing actionable resolution dialog for active operation conflicts and the Operations Control Center live mishap alert banner. Intercept HTTP 409 `ACTIVE_OPERATION_CONFLICT` responses during driver deletion or deactivation in User Management, present active route metadata (route code, driver name, remaining stop count), and provide a 1-click "Reassign Active Orders & Delete" (`forceReassign=true`) cascade trigger. Mount a real-time mishap alert banner on the Operations Control Center subscribing to Socket.IO events (`route:cancelled`, `deliveries:reassigned`) with direct navigation to rescued deliveries in the dispatch pool.

Purpose: Provide administrators and dispatchers with full operational visibility, immediate conflict resolution tools, and live alerting to prevent delayed deliveries during driver incidents.
Output: `ActiveOperationConflictModal.tsx`, `MishapAlertBanner.tsx`, updated `adminApi.ts`, `UserTable.tsx`, `UserManagementPage.tsx`, `AdminControlCenterPage.tsx`, and unit tests in `ActiveOperationConflictModal.test.tsx`.
</objective>
